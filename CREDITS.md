## Video walls — animated loops bundled as MP4 (also from owner-picked pins)

Four of the pins above (Saber's Blade, Hollow Purple, Aincrad Meadow,
Pixel Tokyo) turned out to contain **actual animated wallpaper videos**, not
just still frames. The launcher bundles those loops (`raw/vid_saber.mp4`,
`raw/vid_gojo.mp4`, `raw/vid_sao.mp4`, `raw/vid_pixel.mp4`) and plays them
muted + seamlessly behind the home UI. The static tables above for those four
names are the fallback frames of these same clips.

**Who made the animations:** the pins carry no author credit — **attribution
wanted, same policy as the art**: if you made any of these loops, open an
issue and it is credited or removed immediately. The videos live on
Pinterest's CDN (`v1.pinimg.com`) and were pulled from the exact pins the
owner chose; nothing was re-hosted beyond this private build.

## What "live" means in this project — honest version

Two things here are genuinely live, and one family is deliberately not:

- **Four real video walls** — Saber's Blade, Hollow Purple, Aincrad Meadow,
  Pixel Tokyo play **actual bundled MP4 loops** (muted, seamless, behind the
  UI, verified frame-diff live on the TV). This is the real "live wallpaper"
  feature, and it is these four only.
- **Three code-drawn live scenes** — `midnight`, `ink`, `void`: their entire
  scene is runtime-drawn and animated every frame (clouds over the moon,
  drifting starfields, breathing mist).
- **The remaining art is static images** — what runs on them is the launcher's
  **ambience layer** (Ken-Burns drift, petals, embers, rain, fireflies, comets,
  per-style effects, rendered on the Canvas every frame). We never call those
  static images "live" — the honest word is the table in
  [WALLPAPERS.md](WALLPAPERS.md).
